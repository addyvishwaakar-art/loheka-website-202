About LOHEKA

50+ Years of Excellence in Iron & Steel

Jatan Enterprises, founded in 1970 by the visionary Late Shri Ram Jatan Vishwaakar, has been a cornerstone of trust, excellence, and innovation in the iron and steel industry for over five decades.

After the passing of Late Shri Ram Jatan Vishwaakar in 2019, the business was carried forward by his son, Mr. Mithlesh Vishwaakar, who introduced modern manufacturing techniques and streamlined operations.

Today, under the dynamic leadership of Aditya Vishwaakar, Jatan Enterprises continues to grow and innovate, operating through its flagship sub-brands LOHEKA and LOHEKA FM.
